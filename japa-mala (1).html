🙏 How to Use

A quiet space to count your japa. Here's all you need to know.

①Select your counting key in Settings, or just use Space.
②Set your target — 108 is traditional, or choose your own.
③Begin chanting your mantra quietly, at your own pace.
④Press, tap the mala, or hold the key to count continuously.
⑤Watch each bead light up as the mala turns with you.
⑥When your round is complete, a temple bell will ring.
